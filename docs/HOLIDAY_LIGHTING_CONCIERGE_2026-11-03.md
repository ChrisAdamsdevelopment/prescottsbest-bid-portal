## Business differentiator
PBC should sell convenience + clear design + owner-reviewed installation, not just ladders and lights.
- Customer sees preconfigured classic, welcoming, showcase and permanent smart lighting concepts.
- Private on-photo customizer: local-only house photo, route points, selectable colors/accents; photo never leaves browser unless customer chooses to attach it to an email.
- Deterministic conceptual material worksheet and price research range, NOT AI-generated pricebook.
- Contractor confirms actual layout, permission, licensing, ladder access, outdoor-rated mounting/power, product/model compatibility and service terms, then publishes approved proposal.
- Add yearly maintenance/takedown/storage options, but do not promise to include them until scope and expenses validated.
- Staff installation packet to reduce crew ambiguity; a working lead supervises and validates all distances/risk.

## Unit economics hypothesis — Andrew to fill supplier prices
Use actual material receipt + labor burden + insurance/workers comp + equipment/travel + callbacks/returns + takedown/service obligations and payment processing.
Target initial direct-cost gross margin >=35%, but validate with actual data; cannot infer margins from list prices.
Formula: Gross margin = (collected revenue excluding pass-through sales tax − direct job cost) / collected revenue.
- At a $799 hypothetical install, maximum direct cost for 35% GM = $519. Example costs $200 materials + $220 loaded labor + $65 travel/support = $485, 39.3% GM if true.
- At $1,199, cost ceiling $779. Example $300 material + $300 labor + $100 support = $700, 41.6%.
- At $1,799, ceiling $1,169. Example $460 material + $520 labor + $120 support = $1,100, 38.9%.
- At 150ft × $27/ft = $4,050 permanent install, cost ceiling $2,633. Hypothetical $1,500 material + $900 labor + $200 warranty/travel = $2,600, 35.8%.
None of the above costs are real wholesale quotes. If winter weather, roof complexity, extra trips or product failure pushes cost beyond ceiling, reprice or decline project. Compare ownership model carefully: local rental competitors own lights and spread material investment over seasons, PBC customer-owned sale model does not.

## Crew packet minimum
- Private job ID, scope, owner-reviewed version, customer-controlled image permission, consented property address in staff-only system.
- Annotated photo with numbered segments, physical segment lengths measured in person, product model/SKU, approved cable routing, clip type, fastener restrictions and weatherproof components.
- Height, fall hazard, overhead line, weather, surface condition, electrical load/ground-fault/outdoor connection risk review before assigning worker; no roof climbing based on AI-generated instructions.
- Supervisor verifies license classification, company insurance covers exterior ladders/lighting, workers compensation and any necessary electrical subcontract work. Arizona ROC has separate license classifications and generally requires appropriately licensed trades for regulated electrical scope. https://roc.az.gov/license-classifications
- Staging: inventory and system test, install under qualified lead, nighttime scene check, photos with homeowner permission, customer instructions and confirmed takedown/warranty responsibility.
- Crew QA signature and actual hours/materials; update pricebook and quote assumptions from observed job outcomes.

## November 3 follow-on implementation: product types + consented Gemini
The prototype now distinguishes C9-style temporary lights, outdoor-rated rope lighting, icicle strands, smart RGBIC/neon-flex, and permanent track/puck systems. It does not project a price from C9 assumptions for specialty products; those display "No price example" pending an actual supplier/model-specific quote.

Each two-point-plus traced roofline produces numbered L1/L2/etc route segments with an optional proportional length allocation when a homeowner gives an approximate overall run length. These allocated lengths are visual ratios only and are emphatically NOT installer measurements, manufacturer cut lengths or safe power calculations.

An opt-in "Ask AI for design ideas" control sends only selected package, palette, product preference, approximate footage and accents (no photo or contact information) to the existing protected server-side Gemini API. It generates style and planning ideas, not prices, purchase orders, installation/electrical steps, or automatic design changes. Human staff must approve everything.

Mobile/desktop real-browser test repeated after this second iteration with mocked Gemini: 9/9 pass; automated Node suite includes holiday routes, privacy/noindex and existing private proposal security. Separate production check needed after push.
