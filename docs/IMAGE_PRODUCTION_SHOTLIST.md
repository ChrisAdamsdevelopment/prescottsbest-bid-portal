# Prescott’s Best — Image Production Plan (Future Sessions)
Status: design briefs only; no new images generated during this build. User logo already added as a web-ready asset.

## Direction
Warm, practical Northern Arizona remodeling. Real homes, daylight, attainable finishes, thoughtful carpentry; avoid unrealistic luxury and fictitious proof of work. All AI images marked concept, not built projects.

## Session 1 — Core service images
1. Homepage 16:9 1920x1080: realistic midrange Prescott Valley kitchen, warm light, room at left for headline. Prefer authentic completed PBC photo.
2. Bathroom 4:3 1200x900: practical updated bath and walk-in shower, realistic plumbing and finishes.
3. Kitchen 4:3: cabinet-and-counter renovation with restrained hardware.
4. Shower conversion before/after 16:9: exactly same camera position, doors, vanity and windows; mark AI proposed.
5. Garage/room conversion 16:9: realistic room geometry; no claim of legal living-space status.
6. Finish carpentry 3:2: accurate white casing, baseboard and joinery.

## Session 2 — Sales and explainer images
7. Owner-customer consultation 4:3: warm conversation at kitchen table, no staged age stereotypes.
8. Estimator: square side-by-side practical, standard, premium materials with real SKU references when chosen.
9. Pricing models: editable vector fixed vs cost-plus explanation, no misleading numeric claims.
10. Process: actual job photographs for measuring, selections, scope, handoff.
11. Permit resources: editable, accurate jurisdiction decision diagram verified with official agencies.
12. Prescott-area exterior: wide high-desert home setting without identifying private residences.
